<h3 align="center">Coffee, code, repeat.</h3>

Most of my days go into React and Next.js apps where a page has to feel instant on a mid-range phone, not just on my laptop. I spend a lot of time in design systems built on Tailwind and Vite, trying to make the components other people reach for actually pleasant to use. The part I keep coming back to is the unglamorous accessibility work — focus rings, keyboard paths, contrast that holds up — because a component I can't drive from the keyboard isn't finished yet.
